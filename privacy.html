Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy describes how «Jojajon Uploader» (the “App”), operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Jo‘jajon va do‘stlari, handles data.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the channel owner to manage their own YouTube channel. It has no public users and does not collect data about viewers or children.

What data the App accesses

  • With the owner's consent (Google OAuth), the App accesses the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services: it uploads the owner's own videos, sets thumbnails, captions, playlists and schedules, and reads the channel's own statistics (YouTube Analytics).
  • The App does not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish and analyse the owner's own videos.
  • The OAuth token and statistics reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Statistics reports are kept only as long as the owner needs them for planning new videos and can be deleted at any time.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After that the App can no longer access the channel, and the locally stored token is deleted.
